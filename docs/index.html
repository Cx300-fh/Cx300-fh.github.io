02 / A LITTLE CONTEXT

About me.

From perception to prediction,
and from simulation to the real world.

My research interests connect computer vision, world models, and embodied intelligence. I’m currently working with Prof. Yueqi Duan’s group at Tsinghua University on video generation and world models.

Alongside research, I’ve built and trained navigation policies for quadruped robots, explored time-series forecasting with large language models, and worked on perception experiments. I also help produce stories with Tsinghua’s student media team.
